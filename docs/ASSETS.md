### People photography usage

Each selected Pexels source identifies its photograph as free under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/), which permits website and app use. Credit links are retained for transparent sourcing. Follow its restrictions, including its prohibition on implying endorsement by the people pictured.

The eldercare category uses an older Black man's portrait with a literal description; the photograph does not show a caregiver or a care procedure. The security photo is originally monochrome and depicts two guards at a metal gate. No Rwanda location is claimed for any of these stock people photographs.

Pexels photographs use compressed 1200-pixel JPEG downloads. The Rwanda image retains building and landscape detail at 1280-pixel width. All files are served locally. Replace illustrative stock photography with consented local brand photography when available.
